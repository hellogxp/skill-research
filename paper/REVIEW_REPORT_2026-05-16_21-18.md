## 一句话总结

本文形式化定义了Compositional Skill Routing问题，提出SkillWeaver框架和Skill-Aware Decomposition (SAD)技术，通过检索增强的跨阶段反馈环使7B模型超越72B vanilla（CatR@1: 54.2% vs 36.8%），并在200条human-style queries上验证了SAD的泛化性（DA: 33.5%→64.0%, +91%）。

1. **Q1**: The 200 "human-style" queries are generated by qwen-max. How do you ensure they don't inadvertently encode skill-related biases from the LLM's training data?
