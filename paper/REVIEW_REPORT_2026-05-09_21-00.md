## 新增内容评审

### Appendix E: 14B Anomaly Mitigation Strategies ✅

**评价**: 新增"Mitigation strategies"段落，提出两种解决方案：
1. Constrained decoding (grammar-based generation)
2. Adaptive prompt complexity

**优点**: 从单纯分析升级为可操作的解决方案，增强论文贡献

**建议**: 若能在future work中提及已初步验证其中一种方法有效，将更有说服力

### Appendix F: Hard Query Hint Distribution Analysis ✅✅

**评价**: 这是本次更新的最大亮点

**新增关键数据**:
- Hard queries hint coverage: 48.8% (vs. 90% easy, 80% medium)
- 7/10 hard queries exhibit category starvation
- Oracle experiment: category-stratified sampling yields +12.6pp

**优点**: 
1. 定量验证了"hint dilution"机制
2. Oracle实验证明改进空间存在
3. 直接回应了上次评审的P1问题

**建议**: 无，这是高质量的mechanistic analysis

### Appendix G: Text Overlap Impact on SAD Gains ✅✅

**评价**: 完整回答了SAD增益是否由文本重叠驱动的质疑

**新增关键数据**:
- Low overlap (<0.7): +18.2pp SAD gain
- Medium overlap (0.7-0.9): +19.8pp
- High overlap (>0.9): +21.9pp
- Human queries (ROUGE-L=0.31): +12.0pp (63.8% relative gain)

**优点**: 
1. 证明SAD增益在所有overlap层级一致
2. Human queries低重叠环境下仍有63.8%相对增益
3. 完全解决了Text Overlap问题

### Appendix H: DAG Planner Clarification ✅

**评价**: 明确DAG planner与主指标的关系

**新增关键表述**:
> "The DAG planner operates downstream of skill selection... it does not affect CatR@1 or Chain_cat... Its contribution is orthogonal: improving execution ordering rather than skill selection accuracy."

**优点**: 澄清了DAG planner的作用范围，避免审稿人误解
