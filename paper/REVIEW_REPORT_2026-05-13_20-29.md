## 新增内容评审

### 1. ReAct Baseline ✅

**位置**: Table 2 (Main Results), Discussion

**关键数据**:
- 7B ReAct: R@1=75.3%, Latency=4.4s
- 14B ReAct: R@1=15.7% (collapse due to malformed JSON)

**评价**: 
- 新增ReAct作为iterative planning baseline是很好的补充
- 揭示了ReAct的脆弱性：14B ReAct因JSON解析失败而崩溃
- 证明了显式分解+检索相比iterative think-act-observe的优势

**建议**: 可以在Discussion中更明确对比两种paradigm的trade-off

### 2. Paraphrased Benchmark Evaluation ✅✅

**位置**: Limitations, Appendix I

**关键数据**:
- 7B Vanilla: 99.2% → 96.5% (-2.7%) under paraphrase
- 7B SAD: 98.2% → 96.2% (-2.0%)
- Exact Match: 89.3% → 73.7% (-15.6pp)

**评价**: 
- **这是最重要的新增实验之一**
- 直接回应了"高overlap导致高recall"的质疑
- 证明了recall下降仅2-3%，而exact match对surface cues更敏感
- 增强了论文的可信度

### 3. Adaptive Hint Selection ✅✅

**位置**: Appendix K

**关键数据**:
- Complexity-adaptive: Chain EM 0.397 → 0.483 (+22%)
- Category-stratified: 略低于standard SAD
- Combined: 效果不如complexity-adaptive alone

**评价**: 
- 提出了实用的优化方案
- 证明了hard queries需要更多hints (H=25)
- 揭示了hint quantity vs. category diversity的trade-off

### 4. Constrained Decoding Ablation ✅

**位置**: Appendix L

**关键数据**:
- 14B constrained: DA=0.72, Parse rate=0.98
- Latency: 580ms

**评价**: 
- 验证了mitigation strategy的有效性
- 但只是"preliminary results"，未与unconstrained 14B做直接对比
- 建议补充完整的对比实验

### 5. Latency Breakdown ✅

**位置**: Appendix M

**关键数据**:
- Decomposition: >99.9% of total latency
- Retrieval: ~10ms (<0.1%)
- SAD overhead: 42-51%

**评价**: 
- 清晰的latency profile
- 为future optimization指明方向（distillation, speculative decoding）

### 6. Category-Only Ablation ✅

**位置**: Appendix B

**关键数据**:
- Category-only: 29% of SAD's R@1 gain
- Full-skill: 71% of gain
- Chain EM: 0 for category-only

**评价**: 
- 证明SAD的收益不来自简单的namespace narrowing
- 需要skill-level semantics才能获得chain composition能力
